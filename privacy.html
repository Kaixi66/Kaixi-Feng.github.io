Privacy & analytics

Updated October 15, 2026. This personal homepage uses analytics to understand its readership and improve the reading experience. The statistics dashboards are private.

Basic traffic statistics

Cloudflare Web Analytics measures page views, visits, countries, referring sites, devices and page performance. It does not use cookies or local storage to recognize visitors. Its visit counts do not represent unique people. About Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Optional visitor analytics

Statcounter and Microsoft Clarity load automatically in cookie-free mode on the homepage, unless this browser has a saved exclusion, an earlier decline, or Global Privacy Control enabled. The homepage does not present an analytics prompt or settings button. These services may receive your IP address and information about your browser, device, page URL, referrer and visit time.

Statcounter client storage is disabled. It reports page views, traffic sources, outbound clicks and approximate cities or regions based on IP addresses. These locations can be inaccurate, particularly when a VPN, proxy or shared network is used. Without tracking cookies, unique and returning visitor counts are limited estimates. Its free plan retains detailed records for the most recent 500 page views. Statcounter privacy policy.

Microsoft Clarity operates with both advertising storage and analytics storage denied, and project cookies turned off. It measures page interactions, including clicks and scrolling, in its supported cookie-free mode. Continuous user journeys and some replay features are limited; separate page views may count as separate sessions. Clarity masks sensitive content by default. The site sends named events for paper, Google Scholar and email-link clicks; it does not send a custom name, email address or other identity for a visitor. Microsoft processes usage data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Your choices

You can turn off optional analytics. This stops new collection by Statcounter and Clarity; basic Cloudflare statistics continue.

You can also exclude this browser from all three analytics tools. This saves a local preference in this browser. You can restore basic statistics and restore the cookie-free visitor tools. Clearing site data, changing devices or using a different browser resets the preference. If your browser prevents reading saved preferences, the visitor tools remain off.

Your choices apply to future collection. Previously collected records are not automatically removed by changing a browser preference. Ad blockers and network conditions can prevent visits from being counted.

Hosting and other requests

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Page requests are subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement. Google Fonts supplies the typeface and receives the network requests needed to deliver it.
